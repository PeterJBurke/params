Safety notice

This tool checks selected configuration values, not airworthiness. It cannot confirm the installed firmware version, physical wiring, calibration quality, battery suitability, fence geometry, radio-failsafe behavior, or safe flight. Always verify settings on the aircraft and perform the course's physical preflight checks.
