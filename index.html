Safety notice. This tool compares selected configuration values with the course checklist. It cannot confirm the installed firmware version, physical wiring, sensor calibration, battery condition or chemistry, fence geometry, radio-failsafe behaviour, or that the aircraft is safe to fly. Always complete the physical preflight checklist, verify settings on the aircraft itself, and flight-test failsafes under instructor supervision.
